Skill-Based Labour Market Segmentation and Mismatch Dynamics in the UK, 2016–2022

Job Market Paper · Working paper

This paper develops two skill-based segmentations of the UK labour market, grouping occupations by their skill requirements and examining skill combinations in workers’ career trajectories. Using ESCO occupational skill data and UK vacancy postings, I incorporate these segmentations into a standard labour market mismatch framework and examine the period 2016–2022. Relative to broad occupational classifications, skill-based mismatch measures reveal substantially larger fluctuations around the COVID-19 shock.

Occupational Structure and Occupational Mobility over the Last 30 Years in the UK

Working paper

Using the British Household Panel Survey and the UK Household Longitudinal Study, this paper documents changes in occupational structure and worker mobility between 1991 and 2020. It examines employment polarisation, movements between occupations, and differences across occupations in wages and automation exposure. Approximately half of observed job transitions occur across occupations.

Mental Health and Job Mobility

Work in progress

This paper examines the relationship between psychological distress and job mobility using the UK Household Longitudinal Study. Pooled and individual fixed-effects estimates indicate positive associations between GHQ-12 scores and job changes. Weak-instrument diagnostics and unresolved exclusion restrictions mean that the current evidence does not support a precise causal estimate.
