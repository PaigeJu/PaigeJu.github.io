I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Bristol. My research interests lie in labour economics and applied microeconomics, with a focus on occupational mobility, skills-based labour market mismatch, and mental health and labour market outcomes.

Research

Job Market Paper · Working paper

Skill-Based Labour Market Segmentation and Mismatch Dynamics in the UK, 2016–2022

This paper develops two skills-based approaches to labour market segmentation and re-examines mismatch dynamics in the United Kingdom over 2016–2022. Skills-based measures reveal substantially greater variation around the COVID-19 pandemic than broad occupational classifications.

Full paper available upon request.

Working paper

Occupational Structure and Occupational Mobility over the Last 30 Years in the UK

Using the BHPS and UKHLS, this paper documents long-run changes in UK occupational composition and worker mobility, with attention to employment polarisation and automation risk.

Work in progress

Mental Health and Job Mobility

This study examines psychological distress and job changes using UKHLS data. Current instrumental-variable evidence does not establish a precise causal effect.
